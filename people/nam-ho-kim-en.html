Profile

Nam-Ho Kim is a Professor of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Florida. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Iowa in 1999 and worked as a postdoctoral associate at the Center for Computer-Aided Design until 2001. He joined the University of Florida in 2002.

His research spans design under uncertainty, prognostics and health management (PHM), verification, validation and uncertainty quantification, and nonlinear structural mechanics. He has published seven books and more than 200 refereed journal and conference papers.

Research Areas

  • Prognostics and Health Management
  • Design under Uncertainty and Reliability-Based Design Optimization
  • Verification, Validation and Uncertainty Quantification
  • Structural Design Sensitivity Analysis and Optimization
  • Nonlinear Structural Mechanics and Finite Element Methods

Books

He coauthored Prognostics and Health Management of Engineering Systems: An Introduction with Dawn An and Joo-Ho Choi, published by Springer in 2017.

Nam-Ho Kim · Dawn An · Joo-Ho Choi

His other books include:

  • Introduction to Finite Element Analysis and Design (with Bhavani V. Sankar and Ashok V. Kumar, Wiley)
  • Structural Sensitivity Analysis and Optimization 1: Linear Systems and 2: Nonlinear Systems and Applications (with Kyung K. Choi, Springer)
  • Introduction to Nonlinear Finite Element Analysis (Springer)

With Joo-Ho Choi, he is coauthoring PHM in Practice: A Hands-on Guide to Architecting PHM Systems with Real-world Datasets, which connects PHM concepts with practical implementation using data collected from engineering equipment and systems.

Professional Activities

He serves as an Associate Editor of the Journal of Mechanical Design and Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization. He is an Associate Fellow of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) and a member of ASME, the American Society for Engineering Education, and the International Society for Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization.

Teaching

His teaching covers the finite element method, structural optimization, verification, validation and uncertainty quantification, prognostics and health management, aerospace structures, and applied elasticity.
