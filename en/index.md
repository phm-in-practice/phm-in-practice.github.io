PHM in Practice is an online learning platform that extends the concepts and methods of the textbook to real data and a wider range of applications. It presents PHM implementations for systems beyond those covered in the textbook. Further examples will be added as new datasets become available.

Each example builds on the concepts and methods introduced in the textbook. Readers are therefore encouraged to understand the overall PHM workflow and study the relevant material before starting the exercises. Running the code alone is not enough to understand why a method was chosen, the conditions under which it applies, or the meaning and limitations of its results.

This follows the textbook’s emphasis on implementing PHM with data collected from real equipment. Each example provides the steps from problem definition and data preprocessing to model development and interpretation of results, together with executable code, to help readers design and develop PHM systems for their own applications.
